# 12 · Taxonomy reference

Here's all the vocabulary Dhiti works with in one place: the 18 traits, 4 personas, 4 readiness levels, 6 emotional states, 6 families of work and their 30 roles, 16 industries and 8 capability domains. The other pages refer back to it. The weights that connect these lists (trait to family, trait to role, persona adjustments, constraint requirements, scenario selection) are tuned, and they're deliberately left out.

## Traits

Each trait is scored 0–100, with a confidence of 0–1 (see [Trait assessment](04-trait-assessment.md)). The table is in seed-phase order, which is the order in which each trait gets its guaranteed first question.

| # | Trait | In short |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Curiosity & Learning | Learns beyond what's required; asks "why" and "how". |
| 2 | Analytical Thinking | Breaks problems down; reasons from evidence; spots patterns. |
| 3 | Problem Solving | Notices problems early, finds root causes, drives to resolution. |
| 4 | Systems Thinking | Sees how parts connect and the second-order effects. |
| 5 | Creative Thinking | Original ideas; unconventional angles. |
| 6 | Decision Making | Weighs real alternatives and commits to a defensible call. |
| 7 | Execution Discipline | Plans before acting; follows through; owns outcomes. |
| 8 | Attention to Detail | Catches the errors and inconsistencies others miss. |
| 9 | Achievement Drive | Sets ambitious goals; motivated by real results. |
| 10 | Communication | Clear and precise; adapts to the audience. |
| 11 | Collaboration | Contributes to shared outcomes across different teams. |
| 12 | Empathy | Reads what others feel and need, and adjusts to it. |
| 13 | Influence & Persuasion | Earns buy-in through reasoning and trust, not authority. |
| 14 | Leadership Orientation | Steps up and takes ownership without a formal mandate. |
| 15 | Customer & Service Orientation | Oriented toward creating real value for others. |
| 16 | Integrity & Ethics | Honest and responsible, especially when it's costly. |
| 17 | Adaptability | Comfortable with change and ambiguity; adjusts approach. |
| 18 | Resilience | Treats failure as data; recovers and persists. |

## Personas

Detected in the first phase of the session. The age ranges are rough guides.

| Persona | Typical age | Who they are |
|---|---|---|
| Career Discovery | about 16–22 | Students and recent graduates exploring what fits |
| Career Validation | about 21–27 | Early-career people second-guessing a chosen path |
| Career Transition | about 24–35 | Working professionals switching without wrecking what they've built |
| Reinvention & Independence | about 35–45 | Experienced people building work on their own terms |

## Readiness levels

Detected from up to two questions about urgency. Tone and plan adapt to the level.

1. Just exploring
2. Planning for the next 6–12 months
3. Needing to move within 1–3 months
4. In a crisis right now

## Emotional states

Dhiti classifies the candidate into one of six states to adjust its tone. The label itself is **never shown to the candidate**.

confusion · fear · burnout · ambition · curiosity · pressure

## Background segments

Tech or non-tech, crossed with fresher or experienced:

| | Fresher | Experienced |
|---|---|---|
| **Tech** | Tech fresher | Tech, experienced |
| **Non-tech** | Non-tech fresher | Non-tech, experienced |

## Constraint questions and dimensions

**Three questions** asked of the candidate:

1. Weekly hours available
2. Financial dependency (whether others rely on their income)
3. Relocation flexibility

**Six dimensions** on which each role's transition path is checked:

time · financial risk · risk appetite · learning intensity · relocation · family impact

See [Career matching pipeline, step 3](05-career-matching-pipeline.md#step-3-constraint-feasibility).

## Exam vocabulary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Situation** | One of the 30 base scenarios authored for each role (900 in total) |
| **Scenario category** | A family of decisions within a role; 6 per role, 180 in total |
| **Difficulty tier** | Foundation, intermediate or advanced, roughly a third each |
| **Single-select** | "Pick the best option" |
| **Multi-select** | "Select exactly N"; correct only if the selected set exactly matches the correct set |
| **Regrounding** | Rewriting a validated base scenario into the candidate's industry and target trait, with the correct answers locked |
| **Role pack / industry pack** | Grounding content for regrounding: 30 role packs and 16 industry packs, about 1,100 chunks in total |
